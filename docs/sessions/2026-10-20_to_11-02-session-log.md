## 4. Printability rework (2026-11-01)

The user reported the hinges had never printed: "a part issue before it ever became a printer issue", confirmed by two CSWPs.

- **Diagnosis:** the bodies were clean (watertight, shortest edge 0.64 mm, thinnest wall 2.3 mm). The design was the problem. The snap needed about 11% hoop stretch through a solid ring, and gaps were 0.05–0.5 mm radially and 0–0.025 mm on three faces.
- **User's choices:** an all-printed relieved snap (3D printing is a prototype stage before real parts), and flat, still-modular connectors. The printer has a fixed layer cap; the number wasn't given.
- **Built** (`cad/fusion/scripts/build_print_parts/`):
  - Frames wrap 180° (was 165°/160°), which squares the sockets and removes the connector twist.
  - Connectors are prismatic profiles printed on edge.
  - Hinges are a cup and spigot with 0.41 mm running clearance and a six-finger slit snap post.
- Changing the half-angles in place broke the frames' strap features, so both frames were rebuilt from the updated `build_frames` script instead.
- **Verified in Fusion:** no overlaps at rest, and the hinge moves freely from −1° to 115°. Layer counts at 0.2 mm: hinges 63–78, connectors 95, frames 381.
- Saved as Fusion **v3** and exported. **`e90a8ee`**
